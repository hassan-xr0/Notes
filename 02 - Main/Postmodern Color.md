- Postmodern interior design emerged in the late **20th century**, around the 1970s and 1980s, as a response to the minimalism of modern design. Modernism focused on functionality and simplicity, but many felt it was too rigid and disconnected from the lively world. Postmodernism grew out of a desire to break free from these limits and embrace unconventional ideas and diversity.

### 3. https://poshpennies.com/how-to-decorate-postmodern/
- Post Modern design was copied/inspired by Memphis Group, a design collective founded in 1980 in Milan by the Italian architect Ettore Sottsass. Memphis was all about bold shapes & bright colors, almost resembling a child’s building blocks.

Bold and unexpected color palettes are central to postmodern design, transforming spaces into dynamic and expressive works of art. The _eclecticism_ and _clashing_ of colors IS the philosophy made visual — it rejects modernism's clean, neutral, minimalist palette (whites, greys, blacks) the same way postmodern philosophy rejects modernism's clean, singular "objective truth."
