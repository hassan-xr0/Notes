# Knowledge
A state in which a person is in cognitive contact with reality is called Knowledge. Imagine two poles. On one side it is a conscious subject and on the other side it is a portion of reality to which we are directly or indirectly related. The knowledge of things in form of direct relation is called **knowledge by acquaintance**. While knowledge of things obtain indirectly is called **propositional knowledge** . It is also know as knowledge of facts .For example "You know Hassan" is acquaintance knowledge because you have directly meet (acquaintance) with him. Whereas "You know *that* Hassan lives in Pakistan" is propositional knowledge because you know it by communicating with him or with someone. From above example we came to know that propositional knowledge can be transfered from one person to another, whereas knowledge by acquaintance cannot as you can only know something by directly experiencing it. In short knowledge by acquaintance = *knowing of* and propositional knowledge = *knowing that*
