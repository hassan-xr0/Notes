# Postmodern Technology

As we know Modernism in Technology believed power of science, universal, objective and progressive solution to human problems (space projects, mass production, industrial revolution etc). Whereas Postmodernism in Technology is what happens when these system begins to fragment, deconstruct and turn back on themselves in self-referential-loop. Post modernism doesn't refers to specific set of device or inventions. It refers to the way that technology reflects, enable and deepens the postmodern culture.

Digital technology (internet, social media, and artificial intelligence) has become the primary architecture of the postmodern condition. These technology not only just reflects postmodern ideas but it also contributed in making the society post modernistic. Lyotard's fragmented "language games" can be visible in the form of filter bubbles, Baudrillard's hyper reality in the form of deepfakes and virtual worlds, Foucault's surveillance panopticon in the form of data collection and algorithmic control and Derrida's deconstruction in the form of memes, remixes, and user-generated content
