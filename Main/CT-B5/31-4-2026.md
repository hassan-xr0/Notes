# Flow of Thought :
A boy named Amir who lives in Lahore believes  ***Don't Trust News Channel***. The root of this thought is coming from **Michel Foucault** book called **Discipline & Punish in 1975** in which he wrote ***don't trust the institutions*** but Amir don't even know who Foucault is,  so question how does Amir came know this? answer is clear by media. He watched a documentary on Netflix about how news channel are controlled which was based inspired by a book Manufacturing Consent by Noam Chomsky. But why Amir only selected only this idea not other. Netflix has other documentary and shows which promotes Feminism and gender equality etc. The answer is he saw his surrounding and saw that how every some news channel during election was that Nawaz Shareef is winning where is other are were showing Imran Khan and how some incidents (9 May, Protests , Palestine etc) are not even broadcasting on news as it damages high elites image. 

Fatima who lives in Lahore believe **No one can decide what a girl should do**. In 1949 Simone De Beauvoir wrote book called Second Sex (women's secondary status is not biologically determined, but socially constructed. How society shapes women).Judith butler who is a professor at Berkeley University read this book and also published a book called **Gender Troubled** in 19XX in which she discuss who gender is shaped by society. Then this book is thought  different universities(Western) from where many students and NGO believes this thought and hence it come to Fatima's mind through his peers, teacher (students from those big Universities and now teaching in Lahore), or NGO

These both are postmodern thought
- Don't Trust the News 👉️ **Don't trust the Institution**
- No one can decide what a girl should do 👉️ **No trust in Meta Narratives**
