### Project Overview

We are an architecture studio specializing in minimal and utilitarian design. We need a new website that reflects our design philosophy: clean, functional, and elegant. The website should showcase our portfolio, describe our services, and make it easy for potential clients to contact us.

### Target Audience
Our target audience includes potential clients such as individuals, businesses, and developers who appreciate minimal and functional design. The website should appeal to users who value simplicity and elegance in architecture.

### Project Goals

1. **Showcase Portfolio:** We want a section where we can display images of our past projects along with descriptions.   
2. **Detail Our Services:** We need a page that clearly outlines the architectural services we offer.     
3. **Introduce Our Studio:** An "About Us" page that provides background information on our studio, our design philosophy, and our team.     
4. **Facilitate Contact:** A contact page with a form, our location on a map, and our contact details (phone number, email, address).      
5. **Ensure Usability:** The website must be easy to navigate and provide a seamless user experience.  
6. **Optimize for All Devices:** The website must be fully responsive, looking great and functioning well on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.  
7. **Improve Visibility:** Basic SEO should be incorporated to help potential clients find us more easily through search engines.
   
### Design Preferences

1. **Color Scheme:** We prefer a neutral color palette, including white, gray, and black, to emphasize simplicity and elegance. You could also add some shades of blue or other cold colors. 
2. **Typography:** We like clean, modern fonts that are easy to read and contribute to a minimalist aesthetic.  
3. **Layout:** A grid-based layout will help maintain a structured and orderly appearance. We want to use generous white space to avoid clutter and highlight key content.  
4. **Imagery:** High-resolution images of our projects with minimal editing to keep the focus on the architectural details.  
5. **Interactivity:** Subtle animations and transitions to enhance user engagement without overwhelming the minimalist design.


Our budget for the design phase of this project is $14,000. This includes initial consultations, wireframes, mockups, design revisions, and the delivery of final design files. We expect high-quality design work that aligns with our minimal and utilitarian aesthetic and effectively represents our brand and services.  
  

By following these guidelines, we believe the website will effectively communicate our expertise and attract clients who appreciate our minimal and utilitarian design principles.

Thank you for your work on this project. We look forward to seeing your ideas and collaborating with you to create our new online presence.
